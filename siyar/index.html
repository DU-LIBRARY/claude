      About this reader

      Source text: OpenITI 0748Dhahabi.SiyarAclamNubala.Shamela0010906-ara1, the Muʾassasat al-Risālah edition supervised by Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ. Page tags such as 12:345 give the printed volume and page of that edition.

      The Sīrah volume was added by the editors from al-Dhahabī's Tārīkh al-Islām, following his own instruction. The Siyar al-Khulafāʾ al-Rāshidīn volume was compiled by the editors on al-Dhahabī's method, as Bashshār ʿAwwād explains in his introduction.

      Fact files (dates, places, fields, teachers, students, books and stories) are summaries and are shown as compiled; the Arabic text of each entry is reproduced unaltered from OpenITI, apart from the edition's footnote call numbers, which are not displayed. Arabic texts: OpenITI. Fonts (Amiri, Source Serif 4, IBM Plex Sans): SIL Open Font License. Motion: MIT licence.
