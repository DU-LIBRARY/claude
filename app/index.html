    About this index

    Source: the scanned edition of al-Ikmāl (based on the Pakistani printing of 1350 AH / 1931, collated with the al-Maktab al-Islāmī edition), pp. 5–132. Page numbers refer to that edition's printed page numbers.

    Headings are given as they appear in the book. Where the printed text has an apparent slip, the translation keeps the reading and marks it [sic]. In the printed book the section titles “Ḥarf al-Thāʾ” and “Ḥarf al-Tāʾ” are swapped; here those entries are filed under their actual first letter. Entries 199–200 share one biography in the book, and one biography between 432 and 433 (Ḍirār ibn Ṣurad) is unnumbered.

    Vocalisation notes from the book (for example “with ḍamma on the hamza”) are translated as part of each entry. Saved narrators and reading settings stay on this device.
